Develop a JavaScript program for a shopping mall that calculates the customer's final bill based on shopping amount, membership status, payment method, and customer age. 1. Collect customer information Accept the following inputs: 1. Customer name 2. Customer age 3. Shopping amount 4. Membership status (yes or no) 5. Payment method (credit, debit, or cash) 2. Discount calculation Apply discounts based on the following rules: Shopping amount discount Shopping Amount Discount Below ₹2,000 0% ₹2,000–₹4,999 5% ₹5,000–₹9,999 10% ₹10,000 and above 20% Membership discount - Premium members receive an additional 5% discount. - Non-premium members receive no additional discount. Age discount - Customers aged 60 or above receive an additional 5% discount. - Other customers receive no age discount. 3. Payment method discount Apply an additional discount based on payment method: Payment Method Discount Credit 2% Debit 3% Cash 5% Other 0% 4. Additional conditions Use logical operators to implement these rules: - If the shopping amount is above ₹10,000 AND the customer is a premium member, provide a free gift. - If the customer is below 18 OR the shopping amount is below ₹500, display "Not Eligible for Loyalty Rewards". - If the customer is 60 or above AND shopping amount is above ₹5,000, display "Eligible for Senior Citizen Benefits". 5. Final bill calculation Display the following: - Customer name - Original shopping amount - Shopping discount - Membership discount - Age discount - Payment discount - Total discount - Final payable amount - Gift eligibility - Loyalty rewards eligibility - Senior citizen benefits eligibility For this assignment, calculate each discount as a percentage of the original shopping amount and add the discount amounts together. Do not compound discounts.
